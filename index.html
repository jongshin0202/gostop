About the cards

The 12 month families

Every month has exactly four cards. You match by month, regardless of whether the cards are Brights, Pictures, Stripes, or Singles.

Turn Basics

  1. Play one card from your hand.
  2. Resolve that card against the floor.
  3. Flip the top card of the draw pile.
  4. Resolve the drawn card the same way.
  5. Move captures to your four scoring groups.
  6. Check for Go / Stop.
