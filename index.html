How to Play Matgo

Play one Hwatu card from your hand. Cards match by month. If the floor has a card from the same month, you capture a matching pair. Then the top deck card flips and resolves the same way.

Brights3+ Bright cards score; the Rain Bright changes the 3-Bright score.
Pictures5 picture cards = 1 point, then +1 each. Godori adds 5.
Stripes5 Stripes = 1 point, then +1 each. Stripe sets can add bonuses.
Singles10 Singles value = 1 point, then +1 each.

When you reach the finish threshold, choose STOP to end the hand or GO to keep playing for a higher payout.

This build uses the Korean Matgo rule engine: Chongtong, Shake, Bomb, Ppeok/Jabbeok, Ttadak, Jjok, Sweep, Go multipliers, Pi-bak, Gwang-bak, Meong-bak, Go-bak, and Nagari carry.
