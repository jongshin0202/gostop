How to Play GoStop Online

Play one GoStop Card from your hand. Cards match by month. Capture matching cards, flip the top deck card, and reach 7 points to choose GO or STOP.

Brights3+ Bright cards score; the Rain Bright changes the 3-Bright score.
Pictures5 Pictures = 1 point, then +1 each. The exact February, April, and August bird set adds 5.
Stripes5 Stripes = 1 point, then +1 each. Stripe sets can add bonuses.
Singles10 Singles value = 1 point, then +1 each.

When your played card matches a floor card and the deck flip matches that same month, the three cards remain together as a Pooped pile. Capture an opponent's Pooped pile to steal 1 Single; recapturing your own pile uses its separate two-Single rule.

When you reach the finish threshold, choose STOP to end the hand or GO to keep playing for a higher payout.

Turn Basics

  1. Play one GoStop Card
  2. Capture matching floor cards
  3. Flip the top deck card
  4. Resolve that card

Special Events

Shake, Bomb, POOPED!, KISS!, TAP-TAP!, CLEAN SWEEP!, CONQUER!, 3-BIRDIES!, 3-STRIPES!, 5-BRIGHTS!, and NO WINNER each use the exact cards involved.

Singles Value

Physical Singles cards can differ from Singles value because Double-Single cards count ×2. The Sake Cup can be used as a Picture or as 2 Singles.

Go / Stop

STOP banks the displayed settlement. GO continues for a potentially larger payout while risking a Go Penalty.
